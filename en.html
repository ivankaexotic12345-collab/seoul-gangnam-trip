T-money

最简单：机场便利店购买实体卡并充值。地铁和巴士上下车都要刷卡。余额不足前在便利店充值。

Buy and top up a physical card at an airport convenience store. Tap when entering and leaving transit.

信用卡 + 现金Cards + cash

商店和诊所优先刷 Visa/Mastercard；准备少量韩元用于交通卡充值、小店或机器。付款时选择 KRW，不要选择动态美元/港币换算。

Use Visa/Mastercard at shops and clinics. Keep KRW cash for top-ups and small vendors. Choose KRW, not dynamic currency conversion.
