From Incheon Airport to Gangnam

With luggage, a direct route matters more than raw speed. This assumes your hotel is near Gangnam Station; verify the closest stop once you have its exact address.

BEST OPTION · EASIEST WITH LUGGAGE

Airport Bus 6009

KRW 17,000 / adult

Direct from Incheon Airport to Sinsa, Nonhyeon and Gangnam Station, with no luggage-heavy transfer.

  1. After immigration and baggage claim, go to an airport-bus ticket counter or kiosk.
  2. Search for and purchase 6009; obtain a physical ticket before boarding from the airport.
  3. At T1, use the stop near No. 4 on Level 1; at T2, use the stop near No. 15 in the B1 Transportation Center. Follow the ticket and departure screens.
  4. Use the Gangnam stop closest to your hotel and be ready to collect luggage before arrival.

Time: Usually 70-100 minutes, longer in traffic. Confirm the last departure on arrival.

LOWER COST · ONE TRANSFER

AREX All-Stop + Line 9

About KRW 5,000-7,000

Do not detour through Seoul Station. Transfer to Line 9 at Gimpo Airport.

  1. Buy or reload a T-money card at the airport station.
  2. Take the AREX All-Stop train and get off at Gimpo Airport / Gimpo Airport.
  3. Transfer to the Line 9 express toward Sinnonhyeon / Sinnonhyeon。
  4. Sinnonhyeon is one stop or a 10-15 minute walk from Gangnam Station; walk or take a short taxi depending on the hotel.

Time: About 75-95 minutes. Not recommended with large luggage or during rush hour.

LATE NIGHT / GROUPS

Airport Taxi

Usually KRW 70,000-100,000+

Convenient for two or three people, multiple bags, late arrival or a hotel far from the bus stop.

  • Use only the official airport taxi queue.
  • Prepare the Korean address and confirm whether it is in Gangnam-gu or Seocho-gu.
  • Tolls, late-night surcharges and vehicle type affect the fare.
  • Decline unofficial drivers soliciting inside the arrivals hall.
返
Returning to the airport

For Bus 6009 from the city, normally pay with T-money or cash when boarding. Leave Gangnam 4.5-5 hours before an international flight to cover traffic, check-in, tax refund and security. Ask the clinic about same-day travel if treatment includes injections, sedation or energy devices.

Using the subway

Tap in and out on Seoul's subway. Do not exit the gates when transferring; follow line colors and English station names. Use Naver Map or KakaoMap in Korea.

Payments and tax refunds

Most chains, department stores, restaurants and clinics accept cards, but keep cash for transit, small shops and emergencies.

What to carry

1
Two physical cards from different banks
Prefer Visa or Mastercard and enable international transactions.
2
One T-money card per person
Use for subway, buses and some convenience stores; do not share one card between two people.
3
KRW 50,000-100,000 emergency cash
For transit reloads, small shops, taxi backup and emergencies.
4
Passport when required
Tax-free shopping normally requires passport details; carry it for major purchases.
